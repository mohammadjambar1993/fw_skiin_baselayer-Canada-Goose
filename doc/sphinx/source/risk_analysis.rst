Risk Analysis
#####################################################

    This document describes the risks identified for the main requirement items defined in :ref:`lbl_requirements`. During the Risk Analysis the following activities are performed: identification of characteristics affecting the safety, identification of hazards to people, environment, property and consumer satisfaction and mitigation actions. Risks are evaluated following the definitions in the |qms_sop_risk| document.

    The inputs and guidelines for this document are the following:
            
    * :ref:`lbl_requirements`
    * |qms_sop_risk|
    * |iec62304|
    * |iec60601| 
    * |fdaots|
    * ``[TDB] IEC 80002-1 section 3.4``

    The Risk Analysis is written in an Excel spreadsheet: :download:`Risk Analysis <docs/Risk and Hazard Analysis for SKiin Heat.xlsx>`
